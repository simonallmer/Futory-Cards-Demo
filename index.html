QUICK START RULES

2–4 Players · 30 Minutes

Prologue

The Story

There is Chaos. The Chronomachine lets galaxy leaders manipulate lifeforms and artifacts from different times — and the common folk are fearful and confused.

To restore harmony, and leave the Chronomachine with a single ruler, the intergalactic federation calls for the 24-Hour War.

The last remaining player wins the game.

1
Anatomy

Card Elements

  • Name — who or what it is.
  • Bazaar Cost — the Steam required to buy it.
  • Type — Steam, Landmark, Creature, Artifact, Spark or Destiny.
  • Health Points (HP) — strength and resistance in a single number.
  • Ability — what the card does.
  • Number — its place in the Bazaar.
2
Setup

The Bazaar

Lay out the FireSteam, GoldSteam and LaserSteam side by side, face up. Sort the remaining Futory Cards by Card Number — this forms the Bazaar, open for all to see.

Below the Steam: a row of Landmarks, a row of Creatures, and a row of Artifacts and Sparks.

Cards removed from the game rest in the Abyss; spent Destiny Cards in the Destiny Abyss.

3
Setup

Your Deck

Every player begins with 5 FireSteam, 2 GoldSteam and 1 Ichor. Shuffled face down, they become your Future Pile.

Draw 3 cards to your Hand, hidden from the other players.

The starting player is determined at random. The game runs clockwise.

4
Your Life

Time Points

Each player enters the war with 24 Time Points: a Day Die and a Night Die of 12 each.

Damage strikes the Day Die first. At 12 Time Points the Day Die is removed — that player can never rise above 12 again.

Reach 0, and you are out of time.

5
Your Engine

Future & History

Draw cards from your Future Pile, face down. Used cards fall to your History Pile, face up.

When the Future runs empty, shuffle your History — it becomes your new Future, and what you built returns to you.

Your Hand holds at most 5 cards.

6
The Market

Buying Cards

To buy a card, pay its Bazaar Cost: place the required Steam from your Hand into your History Pile. The card’s Type decides where it goes:

  • Steam→ Hand
  • Landmark→ Playing Field
  • Creature→ Hand
  • Artifact→ History Pile
  • Spark→ played, then Abyss
7
Know Them

Card Types

  • Steam — the currency: FireSteam, GoldSteam, LaserSteam. AllSteam pays as any color.
  • Landmarks — permanent, face up, never discarded. No duplicates.
  • Artifacts — once drawn, playable whenever their effect applies; then back to History.
  • Sparks — ignite instantly on purchase, then to the Abyss.
8
The War

Creatures & Combat

A Creature may act after a full round in the Creature Zone. Its HP is both its strength and its resistance.

Attack any opposing player — they take the damage in Time Points, or block with a Creature. At 0 HP a Creature is defeated; damage it cannot absorb passes to its owner.

After attacking, a Creature retires to the History Pile — another may take its place at once.

9
Your Turn

Phases 1 & 2

1. Steam Phase — buy up to 1 Steam Card into your Hand. The free FireSteam counts as that purchase.

2. Construction Phase — buy any number of non-Steam cards and use their effects.

Whoever buys the last card of a pile costs every player 1 Time Point.

10
Your Turn

Phases 3 & 4

3. Creature Phase — one Creature may enter your Creature Zone from your Hand.

4. End Phase — draw 2 cards, discard down to 5, and pass the turn.

Skip Your Turn: skip phases 1–3 entirely and draw 3 cards instead of 2.

11
Fate

Destiny Cards

If your Future Pile is empty at the start of your turn, reveal the top Destiny Card — and let it happen.

If it touches all players, it resolves clockwise, starting with you. Afterwards it falls to the Destiny Abyss, face up.
