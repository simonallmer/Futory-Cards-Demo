The Game Loop

1. Steam Phase: Secure resources from the Bazaar using your earned Steams.

2. Construction Phase: Play Landmarks and Artifacts to build your engine.

3. Creature Phase: Summon minions to attack or defend your Time Points.

4. End Phase: Draw cards and reset temporary effects.

Card Types

  • Landmarks: Permanent structures.
  • Creatures: Your combat entities.
  • Artifacts: Utility tools.
  • Sparks: One-time spells.
  • Destiny: Game-wide events.
